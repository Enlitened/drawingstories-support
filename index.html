Drawing Stories

Take a photo of your child's drawing, answer three questions together, and get a bedtime story starring their character, ready to read aloud.

Need help? Email csergay@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you're using and what you see on screen. Leave your child's drawing out of any screenshot if you'd rather keep it private.

Getting started

  1. Add your child's nickname.
  2. Take a photo of one of their drawings, flat and in good light, or choose one from Photos.
  3. Answer the three questions together, typed or spoken, then tap Make the character.
  4. Tap New story and read it together at bedtime.

Questions

Which devices does it work on?
An iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) on iOS 27, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.
Cloud stories or Apple Intelligence?
Before your first story the app asks how stories should be written. Cloud stories, the recommended choice, are written by a large AI in the cloud and are richer, funnier and use more of what your child said. Apple Intelligence writes shorter, simpler stories with Apple's own AI. Only text is sent, never the drawing. You can switch at any time in Settings › Story writer. See the Privacy Policy.
Plans and free trials
Your first 3 cloud stories are free, then one a week. Plus (45 new stories a month), Family (120, for up to 4 children; with Family Sharing, each other family member also gets 30 a month of their own) and Storyteller (250) are monthly or yearly subscriptions; Plus and Family start with 7 days free. Rereading a saved story never uses one. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
A story wasn't right for my child
Please tell us. Tap Report this story on its last page, or touch and hold it in the Library, or email us. We reply within two days. See Using Drawing Stories safely.
Keeping drawings on more than one device
Turn on iCloud Sync in the app's Settings. Your drawings and stories are kept in your family's own iCloud, which we can't see.
