Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Your child's drawings stay on your device. Drawing Stories has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This policy says exactly what the app does with your family's information.

What stays on your device

Drawings, the characters made from them, stories, your children's nicknames, age bands and optional birthdays (month and year only), and the app's settings are saved on the device and excluded from device backups. Voice answers are turned into text on the device; the recordings aren't kept. Deleting the app deletes these saves, unless iCloud Sync is on.

Writing stories

To write a story the app sends text only: the character's name, kind, pronouns, colours and the traits and wishes chosen for them; the interview questions and your child's answers; the theme you choose; the titles and problems of earlier stories, so it doesn't repeat them; and the age band and story length. The drawing, voice recordings, your children's profiles and your story library are never sent.

  • Cloud stories (recommended). Before the first story, the app asks a parent to choose how stories are written. If they choose cloud stories, behind a parental check, this text goes to our story server and from there to our AI partner, Anthropic, whose model writes the story. Names of people and places in your child's answers are swapped out first, where the app can find them; the names you give the characters are sent as they are.
  • Apple Intelligence. If a parent chooses Apple Intelligence instead, or cloud stories can't be reached, the text goes to Apple's Private Cloud Compute or the story is written on the device itself. Apple processes it under Apple's privacy policy and doesn't keep it.

Our story server runs on Cloudflare. It doesn't store the story text or your child's words. It sees your device's internet address and an anonymous App Attest key, which proves the request comes from the real app. It keeps only short-lived rate-limit counters (on a one-way hash, not the address itself) and the anonymous key, which it forgets after 180 days unused.

Anthropic doesn't train its models on this text and keeps it only for a limited time for safety checks, under Anthropic's privacy policy. You can switch between cloud stories and Apple Intelligence at any time in Settings.

iCloud Sync (optional)

iCloud Sync is off until a parent turns it on. When it's on, drawings and stories are also kept in your family's own private iCloud, so your other devices have them. Apple stores them; we can't see them. Names, words and stories are end-to-end encrypted, and with Advanced Data Protection the pictures are too. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device; Remove from iCloud deletes the iCloud copy. Shared albums you create are shared only with the people you invite.

Sharing and backups

A drawing or story leaves the device only when a parent shares it, shares an album or exports a backup, and then only to wherever the parent sends it. We never see it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and story packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

To count cloud stories against your plan, the app sends our story server Apple's signed record of your subscription and of any story packs (which plan, when it renews, and an anonymous transaction number; no name or contact details), and a random allowance ID that the app creates and keeps in the device's Keychain, so a reinstall doesn't reset the free stories. The server keeps, for each allowance, only how many stories have been used this month and which purchases have been credited. None of this is linked to who you are, used for tracking or shared with anyone else.

Children

Drawing Stories is made for parents to use with their children. Story writing that leaves the device uses cloud stories only after a parent chooses them, or Apple Intelligence; purchases and settings sit behind a parental check; and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the story text described above, which we don't keep.

Diagnostic report

To help fix problems, the app keeps a small log of errors it runs into and the crash and hang reports Apple's system gathers for it, on the device only. It holds no drawings, names, answers or stories, and nothing is sent automatically. In Settings › Diagnostic Report you can read the whole report, share or copy it if you choose to send it to us, or delete it.

What Drawing Stories does not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address.
  • No analytics or usage measurement, and no crash or error reports sent without you.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data sold or shared for any other purpose.

Feedback and story reports

If you send feedback through TestFlight or by email, we receive what you send, including any screenshot. If you report a story from the app, your mail app opens with the story's text and how it was written; we receive only what you choose to send, never the drawing, and keep it in our support inbox only to answer you and improve the app. Please leave drawings out of screenshots if you'd rather keep them private.

Your choices

Switch story writers or turn iCloud Sync off at any time in Settings. Delete a story, character or drawing in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.
