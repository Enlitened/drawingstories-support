Using Drawing Stories safely

A short guide for grown-ups, with a few things to say to your child. The same guide is in the app, in Settings › Using Drawing Stories safely.

Made for reading together

Drawing Stories is for a grown-up and a child together. A grown-up sets it up, answers the questions with the child, and reads the story aloud.

Your child never chats with an AI. They answer three questions about their drawing, and the app writes a story from those answers.

Stories are written by AI

Every story is written by an AI model, and every story is checked automatically before you see it. AI can still get things wrong, so you may like to read a new story to yourself first.

If a story isn't right for your child, don't read it. Report it and we'll look at it.

Keep answers simple

Use first names or nicknames. Leave out surnames, addresses, schools and anything you'd rather keep private. The app swaps out names of people and places where it can find them, but it can't find every one.

With cloud stories on, the answers go as text to our AI partner, Anthropic. The drawing, voice recordings and your family's profiles stay on your device. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Things to say to your child

  • “A computer helps us write these stories. It's clever, but it isn't a person, and sometimes it gets things wrong.”
  • “If a story ever makes you feel worried, scared or sad, tell me, and we'll stop.”
  • “We only use first names in our answers. Where we live and your school stay private.”
  • “The settings are for grown-ups. Ask me if you want to change something.”

Grown-up controls

Settings, sharing, deleting and purchases ask a grown-up to press and hold first. Turning on cloud stories asks a grown-up to answer a question too. You can switch cloud stories off at any time in Settings › Story writer, and delete everything in Settings › Delete all data.

Reporting a story. Tap Report this story on a story's last page, or touch and hold the story in the Library. Your mail app opens with the story's text and how it was written; you can change or remove anything before sending, and the drawing isn't included. Or email csergay@gmail.com. We read every report and reply within two days.
